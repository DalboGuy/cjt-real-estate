The CJT Partners

A historic Galveston home, restored with care and meant to be shared.

Sand & Sea Manor is more than a rental property to us. It’s a home we took great pride in restoring and bringing back to life while preserving the character that makes historic Galveston so special.

We’re a family-run group of partners who wanted to create the kind of place we would choose for our own families and friends—a home where everyone can stay together, spend time together, and make lasting memories.

From the original character of the house to the gathering spaces, outdoor areas, hot tub, and fire-pit area, every part of Sand & Sea Manor has been shaped around the way we like to travel: comfortably, together, and with room to slow down and enjoy the people we’re with.

We built a home that we genuinely love, and one of the most rewarding parts of owning it has been getting to share it with other families, friends, and groups visiting Galveston.

We hope Sand & Sea Manor feels a little less like a rental and a little more like your own Galveston home while you’re here.

— The CJT Partners
