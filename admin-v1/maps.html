Pricing control stack

What can be changed today—and what still needs a rule

Mixed maturity
BuiltFees & quote settingsCleaning, tax, capacity, published-through date, weekend-day definition, deposit percentage, and split threshold save through /api/pricing.
BuiltSeason and event ratesNamed date bands store weekday rate, weekend rate, and minimum nights. Overlap is rejected.
PartialBase pricingEvery quoteable date is priced by a season. There is no separate default base-rate field or dedicated “Save base pricing” action yet.
Present, not wiredDate overridespricing_overrides exists in the reconciled database, but the current pricing editor, API, and quote path do not consume it.
Present, not wiredMidweek & extended-stay offersLegacy midweek_offer, long_stay_offer, and pricing_rules config keys were found; the active quote engine does not apply them.
BuiltReservation adjustmentAn owner can replace the lodging subtotal on an open direct reservation; supported totals are recalculated and the change is stored as an event.
Not builtPricing file importCSV/XLSX upload still needs a template, dry-run preview, row errors, conflict policy, and atomic publish step.
Not builtCost-aware channel discountsMonthly operating costs, per-stay allocation, host-site fees, discount scenarios, floor price, contribution margin, and break-even are not modeled.
Decision needed · Quote precedence before discounts and overrides
1
Choose a nightly sourceBase fallback, season/event rate, then an approved date-specific override.
2
Apply eligible offersDefine whether midweek, extended stay, coupons, and channel discounts stack or compete.
3
Enforce the floorCompare net revenue after channel/transaction fees with allocated operating cost and minimum margin.
4
Add fees and taxCalculate the guest-facing all-in total from one server-side rule set.
5
Snapshot the resultStore inputs, applied rules, savings, floor check, and exact total on the booking event.

This is the proposed decision order—not current quote behavior. Approve the stacking and floor rules before implementation.

Build status & omissions

What happens next—and what was easy to forget

This ledger separates the active sequence from parked decisions and later platform work.

Living queue

Active sequence

  1. Guest → owner acceptanceVerify request, indefinite lock, trip/pet/event display, and no Production write.
  2. Owner Calendar reorganizationMake the calendar dashboard easier to scan, navigate, and act from.
  3. Booking-page visual acceptanceComplete desktop, mobile, anonymous, content, and 14-guest evidence.

Parked decisions

  • Stripe payment testing and activationCode exists; no test or live charge until explicitly resumed.
  • Final booking sequenceChoose the policy for approval, contract, payment, and confirmation.
  • OpenSign auto-sendToday it sends only on an explicit owner contract action.
  • Production cutoverreorg/platform-v1 stays separate from main until approved.

Missing capabilities

  • Pricing file importNo CSV/XLSX upload contract or preview-before-save pipeline yet.
  • Discounts & date overridesLegacy records exist, but the current pricing editor and quote engine do not apply them; stacking and floor rules remain unapproved.
  • Cost / host-site discount modelNo operating-cost, channel-fee, discount, or margin engine yet.
  • Owner profileContact/photo/identity presentation has not been completed.
  • Two-way channel managementNo outbound OTA rates, inventory, or unified messaging.
  • Signed document indexNo cloud-linked agreement repository on each reservation.
  • Cancellation/refund workflowPolicy, money, date release, event, and message are not one verified operation.
  • Multi-property authorizationRoles, invitations, and property-scoped enforcement remain deferred.
  • Operational monitoringNo single alerting view for feed failures, API errors, and integration health.
