About this place

Sand & Sea Manor is a warm, character-filled Galveston home for families and groups who want to stay together instead of splitting across hotel rooms or smaller rentals.

Inside, the house combines original historic personality with practical gathering space: five bedrooms, three full bathrooms, two living rooms, a full kitchen and multiple places to sit down together. Outside, two front porches, a fenced yard, a private hot tub and a separate fire-pit area make the house part of the trip—not just a place to sleep.

The original staircase is part of the home's historic layout, and there are no baby gates. If your group has mobility, pet, event or other special considerations, ask CJT Realty before booking and we will help determine whether the home is a good fit.
