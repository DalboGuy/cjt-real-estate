How financials are calculated

Periods use Sand & Sea Manor’s local calendar (America/Chicago). KPIs, the chart, and channel mix follow check-in dates in the selected range.

Gross Revenue is what the guest paid for the stay as stored: Direct quotes (lodging + cleaning + tax) plus imported OTA gross. Missing amounts stay as dashes — never invented zeros.

Owner Booking Revenue is what is retained after known channel-related deductions — Direct lodging + cleaning, or the stored OTA expected payout — and before operating expenses. It is not Net Income or NOI. Guest taxes are not treated as owner booking revenue.

Channel / OTA fees are derived only when an imported stay has both gross and payout. Stripe processing fees are not stored, so payment processing stays unavailable.

ADR is gross ÷ booked nights when both exist. Occupancy is guest-occupied nights overlapping the period ÷ calendar nights in the period for this one home. Owner stays and manual blocks are not counted here.

OTA rows come from imported Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com stays. Direct rows use reservation quotes. Direct payment labels are Pending, Deposit paid, or Paid from stored Stripe events — refunds are not stored. OTA labels are Expected payout, Paid out, or Unknown from imported payout fields. OTA stays settle on their channel.
