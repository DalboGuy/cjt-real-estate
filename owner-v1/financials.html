About these numbers

Date-range buttons use America/Chicago month and year boundaries. KPIs, chart, and table all follow the same check-in range.

Direct rows use stored reservation quotes (lodging + cleaning + tax). OTA rows come from imported Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com stays — real stored amounts, not invented zeros.

Quoted / imported total is the stay total when it exists. Expected payout is lodging + cleaning on Direct quotes, or the stored OTA payout. Missing money stays —.

Stripe is Direct-booking only and is not live. Pending means no verified payment event yet. OTA stays settle on their channel.
