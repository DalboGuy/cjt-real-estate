Booking agreement

This page is the guest-facing summary for a CJT direct booking. After CJT accepts your request, the owner sends the booking agreement for your signature. That signed agreement controls the final terms. This is not a downloadable contract file.

How booking works

  • Request to Book submits a request and locks the dates for CJT review. Dates stay reserved until an owner accepts or releases them. You will not be charged when you submit the request.
  • If CJT accepts, a booking agreement is sent for signature when the owner marks the contract sent. Signature, payment, and confirmation are separate steps.
  • The deposit shown on your quote comes from the live owner Pricing settings. If your stay is far enough out, the remaining balance is due on the date shown on your quote. If arrival is inside the split threshold, the full balance is due when accepted.
  • Payment collection is arranged separately. Online card checkout is not taken on this page.

House rules

  • Check-in after 4:00 PM · checkout by 10:00 AM
  • Maximum 14 overnight guests
  • No smoking
  • Pets and events or gatherings are considered case by case. Please ask before booking.

Cancellation

  • More than 14 days before arrival: full refund
  • 7–14 days before arrival: 50% refund
  • Less than 7 days before arrival: non-refundable
  • The signed booking agreement controls final terms
