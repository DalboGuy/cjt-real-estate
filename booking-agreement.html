Booking agreement

This page is the guest-facing summary for a CJT direct booking. After CJT accepts your request, you will receive the signed booking agreement. That signed agreement controls the final terms. This is not a downloadable contract file.

How booking works

  • Book Now places a 24-hour hold for CJT review. You will not be charged when you request the hold.
  • If CJT accepts, the deposit shown on your quote is due. That percentage comes from the live owner Pricing settings.
  • If your stay is far enough out, the remaining balance is due on the date shown on your quote. If arrival is inside the split threshold, the full balance is due when accepted.
  • Payment collection is arranged after acceptance. Online card checkout is not taken on this page.

House rules

  • Check-in after 4:00 PM · checkout by 10:00 AM
  • Maximum 14 overnight guests
  • No smoking
  • Pets and events or gatherings are considered case by case. Please ask before booking.

Cancellation

  • More than 14 days before arrival: full refund
  • 7–14 days before arrival: 50% refund
  • Less than 7 days before arrival: non-refundable
  • The signed booking agreement controls final terms
